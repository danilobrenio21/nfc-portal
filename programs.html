Our Country Placement Programs

Specialized hiring pathways for Taiwan manufacturing and Japan's government-approved TITP and SSW worker tracks.

🇹🇼
Direct Factory Deployment

Taiwan Manufacturing & Tech Sector

Taiwan remains one of the top destinations for Filipino workers seeking competitive wages and steady overtime. We partner with premier semiconductor plants, electronics manufacturers, and metal stamping plants across Taoyuan, Hsinchu Science Park, and Taichung.

⚙️ Key Categories
Machine Operators, PCB Assembly, Cleanroom Technicians, Quality Control Inspectors.
💰 Compensation
Mandated basic salary (NT$ 27,470+) + official overtime pay and labor insurance coverage.
📝 Contract Terms
Standard 3-year employment contract with possible extension under Taiwan labor laws.
🇯🇵
TITP & SSW Programs

Japan Technical Internship & Skilled Worker Tracks

Japan offers long-term career progression with Japanese-equivalent wage rates under the Technical Intern Training Program (TITP) and the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW-1) visa program.

SSW (Specified Skilled Worker)

Caregiving & Agriculture Track

Requires JLPT N4 / JFT-Basic + Prometric skill test passer certificate. 5-year renewable visa with direct facility employment in Tokyo, Kanagawa, and Osaka.

TITP (Technical Intern)

Industrial Manufacturing & Assembly

3 to 5-year skills transfer program in automotive parts, metal press, and food packing. Free intensive Nihongo study provided prior to departure.
