Important Applicant Reminders

Please review our four mandatory compliance guidelines before transacting or applying.

🚫

No Upfront Placement Fees

TW (Phil.) International Manpower Inc. does not collect any registration fee, reservation fee, or screening fee during initial application or pooling stages.

🏢

Transact Only at Marioco Bldg.

All legitimate document evaluations, orientation seminars, and contract signings take place strictly inside our registered office in Malate, Manila. We have no external sub-agents.

💳

Never Send to Personal GCash/Banks

Never transfer money to personal GCash, Maya, or individual bank accounts claiming to represent TW Manpower. Official fee payments are issued an official physical receipt at the agency cashier.

🛡️

Direct DMW Verification

Always verify agency status and approved Job Orders (JO) through the official Department of Migrant Workers website (dmw.gov.ph) under license DMW-051-LB-03202023-R.
