The 6-Step Deployment Journey

1

Online Registration & Pooling

Submit your initial details via our online portal or register at our Malate office. No fees collected.

2

Initial Screening & Trade Testing

Document validation and preliminary skills evaluation to match you with active foreign employer job orders.

3

Principal Interview Selection

Face-to-face or virtual video interview with Japanese / Taiwanese foreign employers and hiring managers.

4

Medical Examination (DOH Accredited)

Complete medical check-up at DOH and DMW-accredited clinics to verify fit-to-work status.

5

Language Training & DMW Contract Processing

Intensive Nihongo / Mandarin basic course, DMW contract verification, OEC clearance, and Embassy Visa issuance.

6

PDOS Orientation & Departure

Pre-Departure Orientation Seminar (PDOS), airport briefing, airline ticket release, and flight dispatch.
