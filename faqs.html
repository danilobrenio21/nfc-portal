Candidate Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding screening, fees, qualification criteria, and office visits.

1. Is there an initial application or placement fee?

No. We do not collect any registration, processing, or interview fees during the manpower pooling and screening stages. All initial applications are strictly 100% free.

2. Can walk-in applicants visit the Malate office directly?

Yes! Walk-in applicants are accepted Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM at Suite 201 & 203, Marioco Building, 1945 M. Adriatico St., Malate, Manila. Please bring your updated resume, valid passport, and 2x2 photos.

3. Do you provide language training for Japan (SSW / TITP)?

Yes. For certified passers bound for Japan caregiving and technical roles, our partners offer specialized Nihongo language coaching modules (N4/N3 preparation) and cultural orientation before departure.

4. What are the qualifications for Taiwan factory workers?

Qualifications depend on the job order. Generally, applicants must be 20–35 years old, physically fit, meet the height requirement (165cm+ for males, 155cm+ for females), with high school, vocational, or college credentials.
