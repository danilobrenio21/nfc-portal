Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about qualifications, age guidelines, zero-fee pooling, and language requirements.

Is there any placement fee during application? +

No. All applications are strictly for manpower pooling with zero fees collected during registration, testing, and initial screening. For specific government-mandated tracks (such as Japan TITP/SSW or Taiwan), all allowable fees adhere strictly to DMW standards.

What is the minimum age requirement for Taiwan and Japan? +

Applicants must be at least 18 to 20 years old depending on the employer category. Most manufacturing and technical trainee categories accept candidates up to 35 to 40 years old depending on trade experience.

Do I need to know Japanese or Mandarin before applying? +

For Japan SSW Caregiving, JLPT N4 or JFT-Basic passer certificate is required. For TITP Factory Tracks and Taiwan Factory roles, no prior language skill is required—basic language orientation is provided after interview selection.

How long is the standard employment contract? +

Taiwan factory contracts are typically 3 years with potential extension. Japan TITP is 3 to 5 years, while SSW (Specified Skilled Worker) visas grant up to 5 years of employment.

Can I walk in to submit my resume directly? +

Yes! Walk-in applicants are welcome Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM at Suite 201/203 Marioco Building, 1945 M. Adriatico St., Malate, Manila. Please bring your complete resume and valid passport.
