Data Privacy & Applicant Protection

Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice

Committed to safeguarding your personal data in full compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) regulations.

1 Information We Collect

When applying through our pre-registration forms, job alert subscriptions, or office walk-in screenings, we collect the following applicant records:

  • Full Legal Name, Contact Number, and Email Address
  • Date of Birth, Age, Gender, and Physical Measurements (Height/Weight for factory qualifications)
  • Educational Attainment, Training Certificates (TESDA), and Verified Employment Records
  • Passport Details and Previous Overseas Employment Histories

2 Purpose of Data Processing

All collected information is processed strictly for legitimate recruitment operations:

  • Evaluating qualifications against direct employer Special Work Orders for Taiwan and Japan
  • Notifying candidates about interview dates, trade tests, and medical screening schedules
  • Complying with DMW, POEA, MWO, and foreign embassy visa processing mandates

We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade applicant contact details to marketing companies or unauthorized third parties.

3 Use of Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses lightweight browser storage and essential cookies to provide a smooth user experience. These include:

Preference Cookies: Remembers your chosen language setting (Google Translate) across different pages.
Session Storage: Remembers if you have already accepted the cookie banner or subscribed to job alerts.

4 Applicant Data Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to request access to your submitted candidate records, request corrections to inaccurate entries, or ask for the removal/deletion of your personal data from our pooling registry at any time.
