About TW (Phil.) International Manpower Inc.

Your accredited recruitment partner bridging qualified Filipino workers with reputable global employers in Taiwan, Japan, and beyond.

Who We Are

TW (Phil.) International Manpower Inc. (formerly Flash International) is a full-service, land-based overseas recruitment agency duly authorized and licensed by the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) under license number DMW-051-LB-03202023-R.

Headquartered in Malate, Manila, we specialize in ethical recruitment, language development, and technical skill matching for manufacturing, caregiving, agriculture, and construction sectors across Asia.
